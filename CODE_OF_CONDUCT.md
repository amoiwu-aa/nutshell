# Code of Conduct

Nutshell aims to be a welcoming and constructive open-source project. Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Share reproducible technical information without exposing private data.
- Keep discussions useful, inclusive, and relevant to the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, and intentional exposure of credentials or personal data are not acceptable.
